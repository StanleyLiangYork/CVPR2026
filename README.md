# CVPR2026 Poster Paper
# On the Trade-offs of LLM-Augmented Multimodal Foundation Models for Clinical Image Classification 
# Zhaohui Liang, Niccolo Marini, Sivaramakrishnan Rajaraman, Zhiyun Xue, Sameer Antani
# National Library of Medicine | National Institutes of Health

# Abstract
# Multimodal foundation models enable joint visual and textual reasoning for clinical image interpretation, yet their behavior under large language model (LLM) augmentation remains insufficiently understood. We analyze the trade-offs introduced by augmenting vision–language models with LLM-generated clinical reports for chest X-ray classification. We adopt an inference-time augmentation strategy in which pseudo-reports generated by MedGemma are fused with image embeddings from BiomedCLIP. We evaluate image-only, LLM-augmented, and LLM-only configurations on the MIMIC-CXR-JPG dataset across 13 pathology labels. LLM augmentation improves specificity, Hamming accuracy, and exact-match accuracy, but reduces sensitivity, indicating a shift in decision behavior. Hallucination-aware metrics, including fabrication error rate and omission rate, quantify how errors in generated text propagate to classification outcomes. Results show reduced over-prediction but increased missed findings, especially for subtle pathologies.
